# Provenance

Target files are imported privately from the user's Japanese `jp.hdi`; their
hashes also agree with the executable calibration records in TH04. That is
cross-project corroboration, not independent pristine-dump provenance.

`scripts/lib/pc98.py` and `scripts/lib/targets.py` originate in the MIT-licensed
[TH04 reconstruction](https://github.com/N0zoM1z0/th04), reused with its license.
Additional toolchain, OMF and Ghidra helpers are adapted from TH03/TH04.
