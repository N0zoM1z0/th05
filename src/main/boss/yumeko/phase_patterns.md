aimed swords (354 bytes), counterrotating spreads (314), and mirrored
spreads (204). Canonical Sword, Boss, bullet and Subpixel types are reused
without changing accepted declarations. Gather takes the real colors 7,6;
both far rank selectors retain their genuine Pascal packed parameters.
ReC98 disassembly supplies semantic evidence, with original complete returns,
branches and ordered relocations checked against the pinned target.

bytes against 872. Its first function emits 357 instead of 354: a genuine

store. The two spread functions retain their original lengths. This is an
unaccepted whole contribution, with no acceptance transferred to siblings
through its failed receipt. The intended whole original relocation stream
also has a nonmonotone first entry and is never sorted or normalized.

Its symbol-only binder supplies no omitted candidate instructions or state
contents. The failed three-function candidate also included the same real
bodies; it received no aimed-sword credit. Its redundant wrapper and binder
were retired during handoff cleanup; frozen producers and Git retain them.
